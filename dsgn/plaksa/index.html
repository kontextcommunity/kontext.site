The Sound.
Sensuality and control.

PLAKSA is a Shanghai-based DJ working the intersection of club and urban sounds. Her sets blur the lines between house, techno, hip-hop and R&B, moving fluidly between them depending on what the room needs.

The vibe is always energetic and sensual, never predictable. She builds tension through groove and drops through attitude rather than formula.

PLAKSA’s sonic signature sits at the intersection of electronic precision and urban groove. Sets are built for the dancefloor: they move, they flex, they keep energy high without becoming predictable. Whether building through deep house into techno or pulling hip-hop references into a house moment, the throughline is always sensuality and control. No wasted moments. Every track serves the narrative of the night.

100+ sets across Moscow & Shanghai.

Originally rooted in Moscow’s nightlife ecosystem, she’s logged 100+ sets across venues including BIZI, Ласки, Изи, Anima and Kultura before establishing herself as a key fixture in Shanghai’s electronic music landscape.

PLAKSA represents the emerging generation of Shanghai DJs: internationally trained, genre-fluid, and focused on building atmosphere through selection rather than technical flash. She’s equally comfortable opening a room with deep grooves or closing it with club energy. What matters is the story, and her track record shows she knows how to tell one.
