the PEAK timeline

from Belgorodto the main stage

Early spark

From curiosity to first mixes

Andrey Glotov got hooked on electronic music around fourteen. Curiosity turned into hours on decks and controllers — practice over hype, craft over noise.

2018–2020

Belgorod groundwork

Local shows and a small electronic project while underage built stage confidence and a solid sense of club set architecture — how to pace the room, not just play tracks.

Late 2021

Breakthrough & residency

After a competitive audition he locked a residency at one of Russia’s largest clubs, moved to Moscow, and switched to a professional schedule with consistent bookings.

2,000–2,500people · weekly club slots

Since 2022

Scale & crowd control

Weekly club slots averaging 2,000–2,500 people and frequent large‑format plays. A key early win: commanding an 8,000‑cap main stage.

2023–2025

Tours & festival circuit

Active routing across Russia and major festival appearances. In 2024 — first international dates in Sri Lanka and Turkey, plus the World Youth Festival in Sochi. In 2025 the run expands to Thailand and China.

PEAK on stage.
Not just tracks — architecture.

Performing as PEAK, Andrey plays Moscow, tours nationwide and abroad, and keeps refining a personal sound and stage craft — turning floors into communities, not just crowds.

Weekly club slots average 2,000–2,500 people. The set is paced for the room: how to build it, hold it, and land it.
