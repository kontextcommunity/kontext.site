01 — Approach

We look backward.

Most of what is called new is old work in a different coat. We prefer to go to the source.

Bauhaus taught that form follows a purpose. The Swiss school taught that a grid is a kindness to the reader. Bodoni taught that contrast is beauty. The printed page taught that hierarchy is a form of respect.

We do not try to get ahead of anyone or invent a new language. We take what has already been proven, and make it right for the person who will look at it: clear to read, correct in proportion, beautiful without apology.

Good design is not a surprise. It is a recognition.

"Culture is blood. Everything else is shadow theatre."
Clear
If the audience has to decode it, we have failed.
Correct
Proportion, rhythm and hierarchy before decoration.
Beautiful
Not as ornament, but as the result of the first two.
