kontext.stage is a live design studio built around the idea that a concert, an installation, a performance — is a designed system.

We work at the intersection of generative graphics, light, and sound to build experiences that cannot be reproduced. Right now we are building our visual language — reactive systems, custom shaders, real-time rendering for live performance. The lights and sound follow.
