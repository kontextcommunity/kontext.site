Why

      

        
Why most visual work is forgotten within a week

        

          
It was built cover-first. A pretty frame, a fashionable typeface, a trending transition. All neat, and about nothing at all.

          
You can't remember work like that because there's nothing to remember — it had a surface, never a structure.

          
We start from the other end. First what it means and what it points at. Then how it looks.

A combination, not a tool

        

          
By 2022 it was clear that AI replaces neither the designer nor the camera. It occupies one specific slot in the chain — and put in the right slot, it frees up time for the part that actually decides the outcome.

          
Since then we've assembled every project as a diagram, not as a task list.

        

      

      

        

          
01

          
Structure before image

          
First we take the event or product apart and see what it is made of. Visual decisions come only after that.

        

        

          
02

          
The tool fits the job

          
AI where it's faster than a human. A human where AI comes up empty. A camera where you need honesty.

        

        

          
03

          
References and associations

          
Viewers are pulled in by recognition, not by beauty. We build in layers they read — some knowingly, some not.
