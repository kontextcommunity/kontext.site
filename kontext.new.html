About kontext.

Not who we are. Where the line falls.

Between art and commerce there is a line. Most agencies walk it carefully — afraid to cross it in either direction. We erase it.

kontext. exists for brands that understand culture is not a marketing strategy. It is the only strategy that outlasts a cycle.

One studio. One set of eyes. Nothing half-finished out the door.

— Our position

Culture is blood. Everything else is shadow theatre.

We are the creative agency for brands that know the difference — and act on it.

Research. Build. Refine. Release.

We don't start with a brief. We start with a question. Then we follow it until the work is impossible to ignore.
